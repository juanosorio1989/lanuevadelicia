# 01. Intent

## Why

La Nueva Delicia has served Colombian food in Jackson Heights for 30+ years. Today `lanuevadelicia.com` shows a
WordPress "Coming Soon" page. Customers find the restaurant through Google Maps, Instagram and delivery apps, but
there is no place that shows the real menu, hours and photos in one trustworthy page.

What makes the restaurant different, in the owners' words: **the food tastes truly home-made, delicious, fresh and
cooked just right, and the service is very warm; customers feel at home.** Every page should make that felt.

Brand line (from the brief): "El sabor de Colombia, cerca de ti." / "The authentic taste of Colombia, close to home."

## Audience

Colombian and Latino residents of Queens (mostly Spanish-first), non-Latino New Yorkers curious about Colombian food,
visitors exploring Queens, and local workers looking for breakfast or lunch. Most arrive on a phone, from Google.

## Goals (in priority order, decided by the owner)

1. **Visits to the restaurant.** Primary CTA everywhere: "Cómo llegar" / "Get Directions" (Google Maps directions).
2. **Phone orders** for delivery or pickup (no app commission): "Llamar" / "Call".
3. **Delivery apps** (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub) as a visible but secondary option.
4. **Events and large groups**: a single "call us" line in the MVP; a catering page is phase 2.

Success: more direction taps and calls (measured in GA4), the site ranking for "Colombian restaurant Jackson Heights"
themes, and the owners being proud to share the link.

## Non-goals (MVP)

- Online ordering, payments, reservations, user accounts.
- A CMS, database, or admin panel (the data model must allow adding one later).
- Catering inquiry form (phase 2).
- Neighborhood landing pages for areas the restaurant does not serve (doorway pages).

## Anti-goals (would be a failure even if it "works")

- Any invented fact: prices, ingredients, hours, reviews, history, credentials.
- Fake or copied reviews, or review markup that breaks Google policy.
- Stock photos presented as the restaurant's food or place.
- Generic restaurant template look, heavy animation, popups, or Colombian clichés in the UI chrome.
- Telling the story through the owners as people: **no owner names, no personal biography, no emphasis on staff
  nationality** (the team is multinational). The story is the restaurant's.
- A slow mobile site.
